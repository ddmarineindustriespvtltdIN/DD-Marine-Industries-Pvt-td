About Us

Where We Build Your Visions

DD Marine & Industrial Solutions is a well-established company with over 10 years of experience in the steel fabrication industry. Based in Auckland, we have a proven ability to successfully undertake steel projects operating all over New Zealand and Worldwide.

Our fabrication capabilities cover structural steel for commercial and residential buildings, metal works, architecturally designed staircases, balustrades, fencing, earthquake strengthening, galvanizing, arc spraying, sandblasting, and primary and top coating services.

We also manage drafting, detailing, in-house pricing, and expertise in manufacture designs for all types of builds. From a small office or residential home to a large-scale multi-level commercial project, we can cater to any size project with the highest quality and safety standards.
